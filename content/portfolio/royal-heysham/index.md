## The Brief
The Royal in Heysham, Morecambe, owned by Thwaites Brewery, needed a new lease of life with a whole new signage scheme that suited the character of the old building.

## What We Delivered
- Timber slat signage with hand-painted wording, finished in deep navy with a distressed effect that gives the signs a weathered, traditional feel.
- A main sign on the building reading "The Royal, Heysham".
- A "Car Park Entrance" sign with a directional arrow.
- Feature boards promoting the pub's offer: "11 en-suite bedrooms", "Quality cask ales" and "Freshly prepared food".

## The Result
The navy signs sit beautifully against the pub's whitewashed walls, giving The Royal a crisp, classic look. The hand-painted lettering and distressed timber add warmth and character, and the information boards tell visitors everything they need to know before they've even walked in.
