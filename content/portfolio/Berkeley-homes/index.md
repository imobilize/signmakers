## The Brief
Berkeley Homes, through our friends at MSA Creative, wanted clean and sleek signage that would add a touch of elegance to their Leighwood Fields development. The centrepiece was a set of monoliths with illuminated details, supported by wall-mounted signs to guide visitors to the sales suite.

## What We Delivered
- Freestanding monoliths at the site entrance carrying the Leighwood Fields name, welcome and wayfinding messages ("Sales Suite & Visitor Parking", "Construction Traffic") and a "Have a safe journey" sign for departing visitors.
- Intricate edge-illuminated lettering that gives the text a soft glow, combined with a luxurious brushed gold finish.
- Raised gold Berkeley "Designed for life" lettering and a wall-mounted "Sales Suite Entrance" plaque with a gold directional arrow.
- Design, manufacture and installation, with every piece finished to match the development's refined palette.

## The Result
The result is a refined, stylish signage scheme that aligns perfectly with Berkeley Homes' sophisticated image. The brushed gold detailing catches the light beautifully during the day, while the illuminated lettering keeps the monoliths legible and welcoming after dark.
