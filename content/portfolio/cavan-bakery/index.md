## The Brief
With the opening of their new headquarters, our long-standing client Cavan Bakery wanted fresh signage to mark the occasion. The bakery has been baking daily since 1929, and the signage needed to celebrate that heritage while looking right on a modern building.

## What We Delivered
- A fascia panel on the dark-clad building reading "Cavan Bakery, Baking Daily Since 1929", plus a unit number sign above the entrance.
- Branded vinyls on the entrance doors and a window graphic in gold echoing the "Baking Daily, Since 1929" message.
- Interior wall graphics, including a statement about the bakery's heritage and skill and the line "Love. Every. Crumb."
- A large printed photographic panel showing bread being made, bringing the craft of the bakery into the office space.

## The Result
The new headquarters looks the part from the car park to the meeting room. The signage is warm and inviting, true to Cavan Bakery's brand, and the interior graphics tell their story to every visitor and new member of staff. It's a fitting way to celebrate the bakery's next chapter.
