## The Brief
Harrow Council's newest library, Greenhill, needed signage for the entrance canopy of its new home in Harrow Square. It was the third library we've worked on with the council, and we were delighted to be involved again, this time collaborating with Objectif Graphic Design.

## What We Delivered
- "Greenhill Library" lettering on the entrance canopy, clearly announcing the library to everyone crossing the square.
- Interior signage including "Welcome" lettering, an "Ask Here" sign at the help point and large floor numbers on the stair core.
- Zone signs such as a bright yellow "Kids Corner" sign, and colour-coded panels on the shelving ends for sections including Non-Fiction, Young Adults and Teenage Fiction.
- Manufacture and installation across the new development, coordinated with the fit-out team.

## The Result
The library is easy to find from outside and easy to navigate once you're in. Bold colour coding lets visitors of every age find their section at a glance, and the signage adds to the bright, modern feel of the building. It's a fitting addition to the new Harrow Square development.
