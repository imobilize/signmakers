## The Brief
KiffGo needed branding for their electric cargo bikes, making every delivery a moving advert for their zero-emissions service. The graphics had to be bright, instantly readable and tough enough for daily use on the road.

## What We Delivered
- Full graphics for the cargo box, with green and white panels carrying the KiffGo name, the colourful geometric van logo and "0% Emissions Delivery" messaging.
- Logos repeated on the side, rear and doors of the box so the brand is visible from every angle in traffic.
- KiffGo decals on the bike frame, tying the whole vehicle together.
- Production and application, with graphics fitted around the box's curves, hinges and door edges.

## The Result
Each bike is now a rolling billboard for KiffGo. The vivid logo and clear green messaging communicate the company's environmental credentials at a glance, and the consistent branding across the fleet makes KiffGo instantly recognisable on the streets they serve.
