## The Brief
MSA Design, on behalf of Berkeley Homes, wanted a striking sign for the Glasswater Locks development that would look impressive both day and night, with a distinctive lighting effect at its heart.

## What We Delivered
- Built-up lettering spelling "Glasswater Locks", with the development's "G" monogram above, mounted on the vertical timber cladding of the marketing suite.
- Double-layered acrylic construction, edge-lit to create a glow around the lettering. The layering adds depth and shadow, so the letters stand proud of the timber.
- A deep navy finish that contrasts beautifully with the warm wood, plus supporting text beneath the main name.
- Manufacture and installation, with fixings planned around the slatted cladding so the letters sit cleanly.

## The Result
By day, the navy letters stand out crisply against the natural timber, giving the suite a confident, architectural presence. As the light fades, the edge-lit acrylic comes into its own, outlining every letter with a soft glow. It's a sign that rewards a second look and shows what careful detailing can add to a development's first impression.
