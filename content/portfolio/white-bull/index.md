## The Brief
The White Bull in Gisburn needed a whole new signage scheme and a general freshen-up with a lick of paint, along with a new logo.

## What We Delivered
- A whole new brand for the pub, with a new logo and colour scheme.
- Traditional signwriting on the front of the building, with "White Bull" painted above the entrance.
- Traditionally painted signs for the car park and gable.
- A hanging sign featuring a painted bull, with "White Bull, Country Inn & Dining" and an "Accommodation" panel beneath.
- New signage inside and out.

## The Result
The soft cream walls and dark grey details give the White Bull a smart, timeless look, and the hand-painted lettering and pictorial hanging sign add real character. It's a fresh identity that feels true to a traditional country inn.
