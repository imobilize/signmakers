## The Brief
Berkeley Homes needed signage for their Information Centre at Huntley Wharf in Reading, and we completed the project working alongside MSA Design. The aim was signage that felt sleek and understated, with clarity and precision at the heart of the design.

## What We Delivered
- A long fascia running along the building's frontage, finished in a rich blue, carrying "Information Centre" lettering and the Berkeley "Designed for life" logo.
- Entrance panels in the same blue with "Huntley Wharf, Reading RG1" picked out in gold, framing the doorway and marking the main entrance.
- Corner and return panels that carry the Huntley Wharf name around the building, so it reads from both directions along the street.
- Manufacture and installation, with the panels fitted precisely into the building's stone and glazing.

## The Result
The clean finish aligns perfectly with Berkeley Homes' brand and with the overall look of the development. The blue and gold scheme is distinctive without being loud, and the generous lettering makes the Information Centre easy to find for anyone visiting Huntley Wharf.
