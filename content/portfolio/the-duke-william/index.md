## The Brief
The Duke William, in the heart of Starkholmes, was looking for a comprehensive signage solution to rejuvenate the pub's appearance.

## What We Delivered
- A complete rebrand and design for the pub.
- New signage on the front of the stone building.
- A post sign at the front of the pub, visible to passers-by and drivers.
- External lighting to improve the pub's visibility after dark.

## The Result
The new signage gives The Duke William a fresh, cohesive identity that suits its handsome stone building. The post sign brings the brand out to the roadside, and the new external lighting means the pub stays visible and welcoming long after sunset.
