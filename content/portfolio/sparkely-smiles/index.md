## The Brief
Sparkely Smiles wanted to update their existing signage to improve their brand's visibility and appeal. They needed a sign that would be instantly recognisable and give the business a more vibrant, modern look.

## What We Delivered
- A bespoke illuminated sign in the shape of a pair of lips, matching the brand's playful identity.
- A custom-fabricated metal body, cut and welded in our workshop to follow the curves of the design.
- A vivid pink illuminated face that glows brightly, making the sign stand out day and night.
- Design, fabrication and installation, replacing the previous sign with a clearer, more modern design.

## The Result
The new sign is bold, fun and impossible to miss. Its shape tells passers-by what the business is about before they've read a word, and the bright pink illumination gives Sparkely Smiles a polished, vibrant presence that sets it apart from everything around it.
