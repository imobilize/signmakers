## The Brief
Mood, in the village of Longton, needed a complete signage package to get its new shop ready for opening. The owners wanted a clean, classy look for their homeware and gifts business.

## What We Delivered
- A whole new brand for the shop, with a playful "MOOD" wordmark.
- Large 3D illuminated lettering across the pastel pink fascia, so the shop glows on the high street after dark.
- New signage inside and out, including an A-board listing the shop's ranges: gifts, homeware, cards and wrapping, stationery and occasional gifts.

## The Result
Mood opened with a shopfront that perfectly captures its personality: soft, stylish and inviting. The illuminated letters look striking at night, the pink frontage stands out on the street, and the brand now gives the business a consistent look across everything it does.
