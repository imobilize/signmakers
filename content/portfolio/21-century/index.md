## The Brief
Century 21 Luxe Residential needed a small but perfectly formed tray sign and entrance panel for their office. The frontage is narrow, sitting between a period pilaster and the neighbouring shop, so every centimetre had to work hard while still reflecting the professionalism of the Century 21 brand.

## What We Delivered
- A dark tray sign above the entrance with the Century 21 name and "Luxe Residential" line picked out in gold, giving the premium look the brand is known for.
- An entrance panel beside the front door carrying the brand graphics and the office's contact details, so passers-by can see who's inside and how to get in touch.
- Design, manufacture and installation, with the sign proportioned to sit neatly within the existing shopfront details.

## The Result
Despite its size, the new signage delivers a strong impact. The gold-on-dark finish stands out on the street, ties the entrance and the fascia together as one scheme, and gives Century 21 Luxe Residential a smart, upmarket presence that matches the homes they sell.
