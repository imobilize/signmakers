## The Brief
The Fleece in Addingham, owned by the Seafood Pub Company, needed a full signage package, covering the design, manufacture and fitting of new signs.

## What We Delivered
- A fascia sign on the stone frontage with raised "The Fleece" lettering, in a slate-blue finish that complements the building.
- A hanging sign on a decorative bracket, featuring a ram motif with "Addingham" and "The Fleece".
- A unique process that creates a metallic look on long-lasting plastic, making the logo stand out.
- Standing signage, plus vinyls for the back windows.

## The Result
The slate-blue signage looks right at home on the ivy-covered stone, giving The Fleece a smart, understated identity. The ram hanging sign adds character and makes the pub easy to spot along the road, and the whole scheme feels as welcoming as the pub itself.
