## The Brief
Chancellors, the estate agency, needed a complete signage and graphics package for their branch. The brief covered everything a customer sees, from the street outside to the seating area inside, all in Chancellors' instantly recognisable red and yellow.

## What We Delivered
- A red fascia carrying the Chancellors logo in yellow, plus "chancellors.co.uk" lettering along the shopfront.
- Window graphics across the glazing, including "Selling? Letting?" messaging, large brand graphics and the branch phone number, so the office advertises itself even when it's closed.
- An interior brand panel in high-gloss red with the yellow Chancellors logo, mounted as a feature wall in the customer area.
- A large printed wall map of the local area, giving the office a practical talking point for conversations about where clients want to buy or rent.

## The Result
The branch now carries the Chancellors brand consistently from the pavement to the desk. The bold red and yellow make the office easy to spot on the street, the window graphics keep working around the clock, and the interior pieces make the space feel polished and professional for every client who walks in.
